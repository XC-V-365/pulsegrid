Creative pipelines

      
1
Sketch beat — Gate-mode paint kick/snare skeleton; switch Vel for dynamics; Prob for humanize.

      
2
Sound design — Drop kit WAVs or keep synth; open FX Labs for bus glue (comp → sat → delay → reverb → limit).

      
3
Structure — Fill bank B as fill/variation; enable Song chain A/A/B/A… for arrangement.

      
4
Export audio — Bounce WAV for DAW/TikTok; MIDI File for piano-roll edit; JSON for ports.

      
5
AI cover / song — Suno or Udio package from Connections; instrumental style at current BPM/density.

      
6
Social — TikTok pack (hook+caption); Faves share card; screen-record grid with playhead.

      
7
Agent / collab — Copilot brief or Composio bundle → GitHub/Notion via connected tools.

      
8
Native port — Export JSON/.pgbin → C++/Rust scaffolds in native/ with same StepCell model.
