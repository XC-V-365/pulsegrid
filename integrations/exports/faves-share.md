# Faves.fm share card

[Faves.fm](https://faves.fm) = human music recommendations (Spotify-linked).

PulseGrid **Connections → Faves** copies:

```
🎧 Fave: {title}
BPM {bpm} · 16-step · banks A/B
Why: {note}
Tags: #PulseGrid #drums #producer
```

Post on Faves with a Spotify link once you’ve rendered/exported audio elsewhere, or share the pattern JSON as a producer note in the discussion.
