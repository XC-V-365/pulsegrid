# Shoom AI export from PulseGrid

Shoom (AI song + music video app) flow:

1. **Genre** — Electronic / EDM / Hip-hop beat (from pattern)
2. **Describe the sound** — paste the Style block from Connections → Shoom
3. **Title** — from export
4. **Lyrics** — leave empty or `[Instrumental]` for beat-only
5. Optional: generate music video with spectrogram art stills

Shoom has no public API; mobile paste workflow is the supported path.
