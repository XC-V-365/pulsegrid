# Udio export from PulseGrid

Udio has **no official public API** (paste workflow). Official downloads may be restricted on Udio’s platform.

**Connections → Udio** copies:

- Title  
- Short style description (BPM, density, instrumental drums)  
- Open https://www.udio.com  

Tip: Udio responds best to short style lines; set instrumental in the app UI.
